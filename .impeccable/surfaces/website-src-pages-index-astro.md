# Surface brief: altvane.com (home and product pages)

Scope: the marketing site in `website/`, which is the home page plus /eq, /dialogue, /compressor and /limiter. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: engineers and mixers deciding whether to try an Altvane plug-in. Action: download it free from GitHub Releases.
Proof: the products' own math (filter response, stem sums, gain computer), the real editor screenshots, and measured
README facts. Constraints: no invented pricing, testimonials, users or press.
Memorable moment: each poster figure is a live instrument computed from the product's real mechanism.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each Altvane plug-in is announced like a concert in a Musica Viva series: one white sheet, lowercase grotesk set
flush-left on a strict grid, and a large geometric figure built from that product's mechanism. It refuses the category
default of a dark hero, a GUI screenshot as the hook, feature cards and format badges.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white stock, near-black ink, hairline rules and faint grid ticks. Each product owns one printed spot ink,
used only for its figure and its own rule. Archivo across its width axis: expanded for names, normal for text,
tabular figures for readings. There are no button boxes: actions are set type on a rule with a drawn arrow.

STORY: The visitor sees exact instruments that are free and open. They believe the precision because each figure
computes what the product does. They pick a product and download it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A top rule carries the lowercase wordmark and set-type nav. Columns 1–5 hold the claim, the download action
and a small poster-style programme block. Columns 6–12 hold the EQ arc figure, huge and cropped off the right edge,
tuned live by the pointer.

FORM: Musica Viva concert poster (Müller-Brockmann), the user's choice; it was 1st on my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK). seed:c804aab7e9a47fe3

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the figures are live (RBJ peaking-filter arcs, stems summing to the input, the compressor knee, a lookahead limiter under its ceiling)
and respond to pointer and keyboard. Motion grammar: one authored draw-in, where arcs and bars sweep from zero with an
exponential ease-out, staggered once per figure, and stay static under reduced motion.
